*Notes:* in the *no transmission* configurations the `NullTransport` discards every event by design, so "Dropped" equals "Captured". In the full-pipeline run the in-process load generator offers about 12,000 transaction events/s to a single-writer SQLite collector; the bounded queue absorbs the burst (high-water mark 2,048) and drops the excess instead of growing, which is the intended backpressure behaviour. A sampling rate of 100% is a stress setting, not a recommendation.
